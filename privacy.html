DiceFlick Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

In short: DiceFlick does not collect, share or sell any personal information. Your roll history and settings stay on your device and are never transmitted. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the app works entirely offline.

Information we collect

None. DiceFlick does not collect any personal or usage data. The app makes no network requests, so nothing you do in the app is ever transmitted off your device, to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

DiceFlick keeps a small amount of information in the standard iOS settings storage on your device so the app works the way you left it:

  • Roll history: your last 100 rolls (the dice rolled, the results and the total). History is saved only while Keep History is turned on in Settings.
  • Settings: your Sound, Haptics, Shake to Roll and Keep History choices, and the type and number of dice you last selected.

This information stays on your device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and contains no personal information. We cannot see or access it.

If you use iCloud or device backups, iOS may include this information in your device backup as it does for any app, under Apple’s own privacy terms. We cannot access those backups.

Deleting your information

  • To delete your roll history, open History in DiceFlick (the clock button), tap Clear and confirm.
  • To stop saving new rolls, turn off Keep History.
  • To delete everything DiceFlick has stored, including history and settings, delete the app from your device.

Third parties

There are none. DiceFlick contains no third-party software development kits, advertising networks, analytics services or crash reporters, and no data is shared with any third party.

Tracking

DiceFlick does not track you across apps or websites, and it does not use the advertising identifier.

Children’s privacy

DiceFlick is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
